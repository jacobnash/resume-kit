# Start here

**Resume Kit** helps you build a resume that gets past the screening software companies use
*and* reads well to a real person. It's free. You don't need to know anything about AI.
You just answer questions.

## What you need
- A Mac or PC with the **Claude desktop app** and a Claude plan that includes **Cowork**.
- About an hour in total, split across a few short sessions.
- Your old resume, LinkedIn profile, reviews, anything you have. Messy is fine.

## Set up (2 minutes)
1. **Download** this kit: on the GitHub page, click the green **Code** button, then
   **Download ZIP**.
2. **Unzip** it and move the `resume-kit` folder to your **Desktop**. (You can rename it,
   e.g. `my-resume`.)
3. Open the **Claude** app and switch to **Cowork**.
4. Choose **this folder** as the folder Claude works in.
5. Type **hi** and press Enter.

That's it. Claude takes it from there.

## What happens next
1. **The basics.** Your name and contact details, and where you're job hunting.
2. **Everything you've done.** Drop in your files. Claude goes through your jobs with you
   one at a time, asks good questions, and turns your answers into strong, honest resume lines.
3. **Your 3 dream jobs.** Paste links to 3 jobs you'd love. Claude works out what those
   employers care about and the exact words their screening software looks for.
4. **Your resume.** Claude writes it, checks it, gets a "second opinion" review, and shows
   you. You change anything in your own words.
5. **Your PDF.** Pick a look, and you get a polished PDF and a Word version, both checked to
   read cleanly in screening software.
6. **Cover letters** (optional). One for any job you apply to.

Stop any time. Next time, open the same folder and say **hi**. Claude remembers where you
were. Everything stays in the `my-career` folder on your computer.

## Good to know
- **Claude won't make things up.** If a line would be stronger with a number, it asks you.
  Your resume should only say true things. That's what holds up in interviews.
- **Short answers are fine.** "yes", "1", "next" and "skip" all work.
- **Already use Claude Code?** You can install this as a plugin instead:
  `/plugin marketplace add jacobnash/resume-kit`, then `/plugin install resume-kit@resume-kit`.
  Then open any empty folder and say hi.
