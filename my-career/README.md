# my-career

Everything about your career lives here. Claude creates and fills these files as you talk.
You never need to open or edit them, but you can.

- `00-inbox/`: drop old resumes, LinkedIn PDFs, reviews and awards here (or into the chat)
- `profile.md`: your contact details and preferences
- `roles/`: one file per job, with every accomplishment we've written together
- `extras.md`: education, certifications, projects, skills
- `targets/`: the jobs you're aiming for, and what they care about
- `output/`: your finished resumes, cover letters, PDFs and Word files
